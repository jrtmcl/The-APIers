How to use Bet375, a betting simulator. No real money is used.

1. View Today's Games

The home page displays today's MLB games, including the away team, home team, game time, and available odds which includes moneyline, over/under and inning odds.

2. Choose a Bet

Ensure you have created or signed into an account. Find the game you want to bet on and click on the odds.

3. Enter Your Bet Amount

Enter the amount of money you want to wager. The site will calculate your possible winnings based on the selected odds and save this bet.

4. View Your Bets

Open the My Bets page to see your active bets, completed bets, pending bets, wager amounts, and possible winnings.

5. Explore Teams and Players

Use the Teams and Players pages to view MLB team records and player information before placing a bet.
