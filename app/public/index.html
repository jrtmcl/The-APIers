How Bet375 Works

1. View Today's Games

The home page displays today's MLB games, including the away team, home team, game time, and available moneyline odds.

2. Choose a Bet

Find the game you want to bet on and click the Bet button next to it. You will then be able to choose the team you think will win.

3. Enter Your Bet Amount

Enter the amount of virtual money you want to wager. The site will calculate your possible winnings based on the selected odds.

4. View Your Bets

Open the My Bets page to see your active bets, completed bets, wager amounts, and possible winnings.

5. Explore Teams and Players

Use the Teams and Players pages to view MLB team records and player information before placing a bet.

Bet375 is a betting simulator. No real money is used.
